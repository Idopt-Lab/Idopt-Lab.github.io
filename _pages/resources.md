## National fellowships

- **NSF GRFP** — three years of support, including an annual stipend of $37,000
- **DoD SMART** — full tuition, annual stipend, internships, and guaranteed civilian employment
- **DoD NDSEG** — competitive fellowship for U.S. citizens pursuing doctoral degrees
- **ACRP Graduate Research Award** — stimulating thought, discussion, and research on airport issues
- **Dwight D. Eisenhower Transportation Fellowship (DDETFP)**
- **NSF Postdoctoral Fellowship**
- **John V. Wehausen Graduate Scholarship**
- **Samya Rose Stumo National Air Grant Fellowship**
- **DoE SCGSR Program**

## Competitions

- **NASA University Student Research Challenge (USRC)**
- **NASA Blue Skies**
- **ACRP Design Competition**
