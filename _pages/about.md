We work on advancing the science and practice of engineering design — improving how complex engineering systems are conceived, analyzed, and optimized.

Aircraft are the prime example: shaped by the missions they fly and the environments they operate in, spanning aerodynamics, propulsion, structures, and controls at once, and held to rigorous certification standards. Analyzing them well means treating them as multidisciplinary systems rather than a collection of separately optimized parts. We develop the methods, algorithms, and software to address those challenges.

### Research expertise

- **Certification-Driven Design** — treating certification criteria as constraints that shape the feasible design space, rather than checks applied after the fact.
- **Integration of MDAO and MBSE** — connecting multidisciplinary design, analysis, and optimization with model-based systems engineering.
- **Multimodal Transportation Systems** — assessing how new air vehicles interact with the wider transportation network.
