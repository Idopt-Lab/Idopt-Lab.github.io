IDOpt Lab

We work on advancing the science and practice of engineering design — improving how complex engineering systems are conceived, analyzed, and optimized. Aircraft are the prime example: shaped by the missions they fly and the environments they operate in, spanning aerodynamics, propulsion and structures at once, and held to rigorous certification standards. We develop the methods, algorithms, and software to address those challenges.
