**Role:** PhD thesis, ASDL, Georgia Tech

Explores physics-based model reduction to produce a simplified beam representation of aircraft wing primary structure, built on OpenMDAO with CasADi automating derivative computation. It handles arbitrary cross-sections, dynamic loads, both strength and buckling constraints, and multiple beam members connected by joints carrying masses and point loads, with a one-time correction from the higher-order Variational Asymptotic Method. Against higher-fidelity shell models, predicted weight differs by less than 5% with a 12x speed-up, even for configurations as complex as the truss-braced wing.
