PI: Dr. Rakesh Kapania

Phase I: January 1, 2025 to July 15, 2025; Phase II: July 16, 2025 to December 31, 2025

As part of the SafeUAS initiative led by Prius Intelli, our group is engaged in developing mechanisms that enable Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) to safely disassemble in flight under controlled, commanded conditions. The overarching goal is to ensure that disassembled components descend safely and meet the safety thresholds required for UAS operations over people in the National Airspace System (NAS).
