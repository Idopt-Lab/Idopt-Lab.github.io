**Funding:** NASA Transformational Tools and Technologies (TTT)
**Role:** PhD thesis work; collaborator on the PhD thesis of David Solano

No previous work had examined the effect of high-level outer mold line (OML), engine, and structural layout variables on structural weight. For the PEGASUS concept, this work obtained a structural layout with the least sensitivity to gust loads specified by 14-CFR regulations. For the Truss-Braced Wing (TBW) concept, it identified the strut-wing attachment location giving the least overall primary structure weight.
