**Role:** Postdoctoral scholar at ASDL, with Dr. Evan Harrison

A six-degree-of-freedom flight dynamics simulation tool whose API accepts any aerodynamic, propulsion, and mass properties model. Aircraft may be defined with arbitrary numbers of propulsion devices and control surfaces. A closed-loop control system supports prediction of regulation-specified dynamic maneuvers and handling qualities.
