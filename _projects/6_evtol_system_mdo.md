**Funding:** NASA University Leadership Initiative (ULI)
**Role:** Postdoctoral scholar, with PI Dr. John Hwang

A first-of-its-kind demonstration of large-scale MDO with seven disciplines contributing design variables to a system-level optimization — 102 design variables and 19 constraints. Low-fidelity physics-based analysis kept turnaround to roughly 30 minutes on a standard design computer, yielding 11.4% lower mass than a baseline sized vehicle.
