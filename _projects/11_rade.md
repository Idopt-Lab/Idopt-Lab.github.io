**Role:** Postdoctoral scholar at ASDL, with Dr. Jason Corman

A tool for rapid geometry manipulation of conventional and unconventional aircraft, covering outer mold line modification and automated wingbox structural layout. It provides an API to interface with physics-based solvers: AVL for low-fidelity aerodynamics, Nastran for structural and aeroelastic analysis, and HyperSizer for structural sizing.
