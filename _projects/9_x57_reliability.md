**Role:** Collaborator on the PhD thesis of Mayank Bendarkar

Implements a safety assessment framework for off-nominal performance and reliability, using a Bayesian decision framework for compliance assessment. A case study on the X-57 distributed electric propulsion aircraft identified battery failure as a catastrophic scenario, with loss of the cruise motor or traction power buses the next most critical hazardous failures.
