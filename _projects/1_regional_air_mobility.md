Sponsor: NASA (Sept 2025 to Aug 2028)

PI: Darshan Sarojini; Collaborators: Dr. Antonio Trani (VT), Marshall University, Vertx Partners

This project develops methods to assess the impact of regional air mobility (RAM) on overburdened regions across the country. We will develop a framework combining aircraft design and door-to-door air transportation analysis. The framework will be applied to perform a comprehensive feasibility assessment of RAM in rural Appalachia from a geographic, operational, and socio-economic standpoint.

## Subprojects

**Door-to-Door Mobility Framework** &mdash; Grant Lee

A transportation model that evaluates a RAM trip end to end&mdash;including the ground legs at either end, not just the flight&mdash;so that a candidate vehicle and route network can be judged on the door-to-door time and cost a traveler actually experiences. Applied to West Virginia as the first test case.

Lee, G., Wang, Z., Hogge, D., Anwar, A., Bhatt, K., Sarojini, D., & Trani, A. (2026). [A Transportation Model for Door-to-Door Regional Air Mobility Applied to West Virginia](https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2026-4065). In AIAA AVIATION 2026 Forum.
