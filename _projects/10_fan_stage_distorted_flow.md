**Role:** Collaborator on the PhD thesis of Manish Pokhrel

Represents the fan blade as a higher-order beam model through the Variational Asymptotic Method (VAM) — the first reported work applying VAM to transient structural analysis of fan rotor blades. Rotor structural analysis covers dynamic stress computation, excitation frequency determination, and resonance margin calculation across vibration modes. The resulting design satisfies structural constraints at a cost of only 0.22% in aerodynamic stage efficiency.
