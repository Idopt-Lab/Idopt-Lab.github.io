**Role:** Collaborator on the PhD thesis of Jiacheng Xie

Captures the trade-off between performance and certification constraints, including 14-CFR Part 25 Subpart B (Flight Characteristics) and Subpart C (Structures). Applications include retrofit design of a Boeing 777-type horizontal tail and redesign of the vertical tail for NASA's PEGASUS concept.
