Certification criteria shape the feasible design space and, therefore, must be embedded directly into conceptual aircraft design, not simply checked after the fact.

This project develops methods to explicitly account for certification criteria, including reliability, handling qualities (HQ), and time-marching simulations specified by 14-CFR regulations into a large-scale MDAO framework for aircraft design.
