Certification criteria shape the feasible design space and, therefore, must be embedded directly into conceptual aircraft design, not simply checked after the fact.

This project develops methods to explicitly account for certification criteria&mdash;including reliability, handling qualities (HQ), and the time-marching simulations specified by 14-CFR regulations&mdash;within a large-scale MDAO framework for aircraft design. Because the evidence available at the conceptual stage is incomplete, the same methods have to carry uncertainty forward rather than collapse it to a point estimate: margins, model form, and operating conditions are all uncertain while the design space is still fluid.

## Subprojects

**Advanced Visualization Methods** &mdash; Tien Nguyen

Web-based visualization of 3-D geometry alongside scientific and timeseries data in a common analytic interface, so that design and certification evidence can be inspected together. [Read more](/projects/3_advanced_visualization/).

**Multifidelity Reduced-Order Models** &mdash; Khushi Piparava

Reduced-order models that fuse a small number of expensive high-fidelity simulations with many cheap low-fidelity ones, making the repeated evaluations required for uncertainty quantification affordable. Builds on our [manifold-alignment ROM work](/projects/15_multifidelity_rom/).

**MBSE&ndash;MDAO Integration**

Connecting requirements to the analyses that satisfy them, so the rationale behind a configuration stays traceable as the design evolves and certification arguments can be tied back to the models that support them. Builds on our [extended RFLP framework](/projects/16_mbse_rflp/).
